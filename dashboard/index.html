The screen — who gets on the board

A protocol is a candidate when the snapshot records no token for it (CoinGecko id, ticker and market cap all absent) and it is not a wrapped, staked, bridged or exchange product of a token that already trades. Five rules do the excluding: R1 derivative category (Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Cross Chain Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC, CEX); R2 the words wrapped / staked / staking in the name; R3 an exchange’s name at the front (a house product, e.g. Binance staked ETH); R4 a version suffix (V2, 2.0… — a deployment of another protocol); R5 a bare settlement ticker plus one letter (a wrapped unit, e.g. USDCx). That is why WBTC, Binance staked ETH and USDT0 are not on this board: each is an old token in a new wrapper. The house watchlist (TVL ≥ $50M after the same screen) is a floor, not a ceiling — every watchlist protocol is on the board, and the board reaches below the floor to the whole $5M+ universe.

The value model — what a launch would be worth

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Peer multiple — the median market-cap/TVL of the snapshot’s own token-bearing protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the family median and then the global median by comp count (weight n/(n+6)), so a category with three comps borrows strength instead of inventing a number. The detail view shows the exact comps behind every row.
  • Growth — 7-day TVL change clamped to [−40%, +80%], weighted 0.30: one hot week moves value by at most +24%.
  • Maturity — 0.80 + 0.40 × (1 − e−age/720): a protocol tracked for years with no token has a record a launch can price; a week-old one does not. Unknown listing date is neutral (×1.00).
  • Breadth — +1.5% per chain beyond the first, capped at six: multi-chain distribution is worth something at launch, not everything.

The model is one function (impliedValueFor in the page, pickMultiple and friends in the program) applied to every row alike. No protocol name is special-cased anywhere. Conviction is a display score only — size, momentum, track record — and never changes a rank.

The farm plans

Each row carries six steps generated from its own record — category action, capital scaled to its TVL tier, gas on its first chain, a holding period scaled to its age — each with a cost and a time. The plan assumes a token, if it comes, rewards sustained real use; it never advises splitting one position across many wallets.
